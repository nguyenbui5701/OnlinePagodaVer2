## 3. Art direction for the only missing botanical pair

Two original, low-poly and visually harmonious assets: **one open lotus blossom and one upright lotus bud** to float/grow naturally around the already approved lake. A restrained warm white-to-blush/pink petal gradient, muted green sepals and subtle warm core; pleasant from close/water-level, three-quarter and aerial angles. Balanced against R16 pink canopy and P0.11 dark blue-violet lake; **do not overwhelm or obscure faces, lotus seats, the golden trunk or gift overlays**.

**Scale lock (proposed art targets to be measured, not fabricated actual dimensions):**
- P02 approved sitting pad diameter ~2.08m; a **flower bloom ~0.55–0.85m** across petals is a small environmental accent, not a second hero. Include an unobtrusive stem base near the lake **water plane world Y=0**; blossom center may rise above the water as appropriate, but state exact pivot/root and height in the manifest.
- Bud ~**0.22–0.40m diameter**, a distinctly taller/closed or pointed silhouette, ~**0.35–0.75m** above water (all art targets). Provide accurate +Y/up, meters, no scene camera inside GLB.
- **LOD budget proposals, not already achieved stats:** flower QUALITY ≤2,400 triangles, ECO ≤800; bud QUALITY ≤1,000 triangles, ECO ≤350. Prefer **1–3 shared simple materials / zero texture images** per asset; double-sided surfaces/normals only where physically warranted. AI Visual may propose a measured alternative with SA explanation if silhouette requires it; avoid needless high detail.
- No animation required on the art side; Codex may later implement tiny sway in the viewer. No particle water, gift sparks, FX event logic, skybox, hills or 12 more characters in this task.
