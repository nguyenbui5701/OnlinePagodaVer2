## 3. World layers and koi
- Foreground: existing real-seat zone and nearby lotus clusters stay readable. Midground: curving channels of lotus, open water where koi can be seen. Background: original low-poly mountain/hill silhouettes in 2–3 staggered depth layers, low islands/lotus beds, occasional warm light accents if coherent with Buddhist pond. No competing second hero tree or crowded buildings; no flat billboard substitution for actual parallax near/mid geography. Distant forms can use lightweight LOD/impostor with documented limitations. Soft violet/pink haze and restrained contrast, horizon not empty; protect tree silhouette, no obvious repetition/edge of plane. Extend bounds/camera far plane only after checking depth precision/fog.
- Koi: original stylized white/orange/red/gold fish, visible just under water, curved body, tail and fins with seamless 2–4s swim loop and consistent forward axis; no floating above surface or exaggerated oversize. Proposed ECO 6–10 active koi, QUALITY 12–18, distributed paths/phase/speed in open channels; low detail or cull distant/off-camera. World depth below water Y=0 with clearance from tree roots/pads and pond boundary, no intersections/teleporting. Bounded deterministic spline/steering, subtle turns/tail motion, no per-frame allocation or expensive full fluid simulation. Water shader must actually expose fish through tinted attenuation or controlled refraction/compositing; correct draw/depth order, avoid fish over foreground actors. Prove motion in native clip, not just Blender render. No repeated visible clipping or school stacking. No gift-triggered fish count, no new events.
- Use approved flowers/leaf assets from B8 for distant density, low-poly batches, keep visible pad/plant counts distinct and no synthetic viewer names.

## 4. Atmosphere / FX direction
- Mist: layered low drifting mist at water/root and distant banks, depth fade, varied size/motion, no white opaque screen-sized quads; keep near gift face/name/lotus readable. Petals: recognizable shapes, slow irregular drift, varied rotations/depth, fall from canopy region and dissolve at water, no static hanging blossom disguised as FX. Leaves: distinguish from petals by shape/tone and motion. Fireflies: small warm gold pulsing lights in near/mid zones, gentle brightness rhythm, no rapid flashes or full-screen glow. Water: existing ripple/reflection enhanced only as needed for fish and distant depth, avoid excessive specular/noisy sparkle.
- Provisional capped total ambient pools (not additive per every new pad): ECO motes 90–140, petals 70–110, leaves 12–22, mist 10–16; QUALITY motes 160–240, petals 120–180, leaves 24–40, mist 16–24. Include seat-local emitters within total cap, not per-pad unbounded multiplication. Tune actual visible intensity with matched shots; if performance misses reduce far/overdraw first. Separate density zones, use seeded phase and pooling, batched GPU instancing/points, bounded pixel size and transparency overdraw. No per-particle shadow or light. Optional QUALITY glow only if measured, ECO retains readable atmosphere. FX reduce locally during gift focus without hiding nameplates, gift icon, or count. Gift FX lifecycle untouched.
- Offer opt-in environment/FX profile under Dashboard visual settings, existing settings saved and offline applied for topology; lightweight runtime intensity only if independently safe and tested, no new hot reload mandate. Default old preset unchanged until Owner approval; explicit OFF rollback. Don't modify audio, presence, gateway except strictly necessary settings plumbing; no new token/port.
