## 3. Optional density/performance strategy

- Do not add additional geometry assets, effects, shadow passes, water shaders, bloom or new camera transitions. Male and female ~17k triangles each; 12 total actor figures is already significant. Reuse GLB geometries/materials; make only **one female source fetch/load** when diversity ON, no GLB downloads when OFF.
- Group far decorators by **model+material+primitive**, camera-frustum compaction, cap instancing groups and draw calls. If all 6 colors across 12 figures explode drawcalls, apply a measured **3-color cap** or weighted palette in ECO; QUALITY may use up to six only if measured viable. Preserve aesthetic variation as well as resource bounds, not unbounded color diversity.
- Do not confuse native Edge/SwiftShader 0.85 FPS with real owner RTX/LIVE performance. Measure matched before/after using same 720×1280, camera/time and profile and report drawcalls, triangles, geometry/material count, rAF p50/p95/p99, JS memory, relevant WebGL diagnostics and render errors. Existing synthetic teardown `INVALID_OPERATION` warnings remain known until the hardware gate, not a pass just because the word 'teardown' is present.
- Keep all existing botanical placements/GLB SHA at PR #11, twilight/gold-water Sakura palette, three actor gifts and screen readability intact. This phase is appearance variety, not scenery, population, real LIVE functionality or eventual P0.13 camera choreography.
