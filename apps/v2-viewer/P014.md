## Regression and source limits

The 85 s idle and 66 s gifts, capacity and community runs use 60 Hz virtual time at each of the three seeds. The runs exercised west-low/east-low in seed 9 and west-high/east-high in seeds 71 and 1337 over two idle cycles, with no selected-route fallback. R16 surface rays tested the sampled camera path at 10 Hz without intersections. The minimum observed camera height above water was 0.946 m and the community pad-bound clearance was at least 2.416 m. The community gift interrupts an aerial move. At seeds 71 and 1337 the largest 60 Hz positional step in that approach was 1.625 m. The approach and return preserve the accepted timing, with the fast aerial descent documented for visual review. The 12-frame approach, 35-frame gift and 21-frame return sections are native PNG sequences at declared virtual cadence.
