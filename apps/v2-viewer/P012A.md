## World and identity

The three original positions, pad rotations, contact and camera anchors are pinned. Nine renderer-only slots span a seeded arc around the trunk. Each has a maximum 0.25 m offset on each horizontal axis. Every figure faces the imported Sakura pivot. The measured P02 radius is 1.067 m. Seeds 9 and 71 have at least 1.45 m between conservative pad bounds and at least 3.88 m of lateral clearance between low sampled camera positions and added pad bounds. Frustum culling and Sakura occlusion mean a single portrait view shows a subset of the 12 world positions.

The three hero positions each use a single shared P02/P07b1 presentation for either state. The DOM name fades in over 320 ms only when a visible, authoritative actor occupies the slot and is in range. The name clears as soon as the authoritative binding ends. Renderers reuse the same geometry, pose, facing and seat contact for both states. Gift focus uses the original actor and anchor mapping. Extra anonymous figures share a breath sample with the existing hero and use up to 15 instanced batches. The extras receive QUALITY shadows and do not add shadow casters.

The scene starts with 12 anonymous figures and 0 actors. Only the isolated `demo=community` scenario turns on `spawnOnMember` and uses a 5 s avatar TTL to show release. It retains the three-seat cap. Anonymous identifiers exist only in the renderer. The room total comes from accepted `room` events. The reserved gift prevents expiry until the active focus ends.
