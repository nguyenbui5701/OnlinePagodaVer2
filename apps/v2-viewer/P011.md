## Look integration

| Look | Use | Runtime intent |
|---|---|---|
| `DUSK_COMMUNITY` | CLOSE and COMMUNITY | Navy-violet water, warm reflected ribbons and a readable actor |
| `PINK_AERIAL_BLESSING` | AERIAL | Pink directional rim and a brighter canopy reflection against the cool lake |
| `GOLDEN_UNDER_TREE` | UNDER_TREE | Warm rim, more gold reflected light and brighter fissures against dark plum bark |

The renderer blends linear color and scalar parameters with smoothstep on the existing camera route clock. Dusk/pink transitions over 14.4–17.6 s; pink/gold over 27.4–30.6 s; gold/dusk over 37–40 s. CLOSE and COMMUNITY share dusk across 9 s. Gift sequences ease to dusk over 1.5 s and return from the current look over 1.45 s, independent of the actor/request that owns focus. Sky uses a two-triangle far-depth gradient in the existing forward pass. Both profiles retain three directional lights and one ambient light with the P0.10 shadow-map budgets.

R16 source verification precedes material integration. The exact `Golden_bark_veins` material is the only cloned material. The source emissive factor in both profiles is [0.35, 0.13, 0.035]; the cloned `emissiveIntensity` is multiplied by 1.12–1.42. Other material factors remain the imported values. Cleanup restores the original mesh assignment before releasing the clone and imported assets. Tests cover shared assignments and disposal; context restoration reuses one clone. Canopy color comes from lighting and fog. The lake stays on Y=0 with the existing P0.10 gift ring independent of look. One shader pass adds broken peach/gold highlights around the reflected Sakura pivot. This is a stylized optical approximation. Existing particle counts, gift lifecycle and world transforms are inherited.

## Executed results

Browser evidence source `9a2f9671ce4aae20cacfe03720c9e7c268d5b1c6`. 30 tests passed and both packages built on Node 24.16.0. Seed 9 and 71 traces kept the largest look-weight step at 0.00850 and exposure step at 0.000319 per 60 Hz virtual sample, including gift and return. The same fixed water crop changed 37,924 of 68,200 pixels between 26.5 and 28.5 s. In the matched ECO under-tree crop, the display-luma ratio for sampled veins versus bark rose from 2.72 to 3.74. This is primarily a gain in contrast against darker bark. The measured veins themselves were slightly dimmer in absolute display luma. The three confirmed cues reused the original gift pool and the run finished at 7 confirmed gifts.
