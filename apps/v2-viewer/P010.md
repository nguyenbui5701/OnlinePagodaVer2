## Rendering and budgets

The water plane stays at Y=0. Crossing warp, broad waves and fine detail are time-driven. Reflected sightlines sample soft canopy and trunk-light masks from the imported Sakura pivot, with a short seat-local ripple for confirmed gifts. This produces a stylized reflection using a single water pass. Ambient motes, notched pink petals and scarce muted olive-gold leaves use depth-tested seeded Points. Gentle visibility curves fade falling sprites in and out as they recycle. The gift pool uses the animated FX/head anchors and the seat origin so particles stay low and the water ring stays centered on the pad's world X/Z.

The counts are allocated point slots submitted by the renderer when their layer is active. Depth, distance and shader fades reduce the number of sprites visible in an individual frame. Idle ambience adds one draw call. A visible celebration adds at most one further draw call. Points share geometry and material, with no added textures, render targets or per-emission resource creation.

## Gift semantics

The visual controller observes the typed snapshot, gift acknowledgement and resolved visible actor/seat. The dedup key is room session + request + actor + seat, with separate observed and confirmed bits. An observed request can produce one faint halo and four motes for up to 950 ms. It never starts a water ripple. A matching confirmed request starts one 1.65 s burst when its actor reaches `GIFT_FOCUS`. Queued B/C acknowledgements remain in the existing driver until their own turn. The 2.25 m maximum ripple radius is at Y=0 and never comes from the overlay fallback. An interrupted effect fades within 260 ms on cinematic return, subject to its original expiry. A new active actor clears the previous cue. Disconnect removes the cue and reconnect preserves receipts. A new room clears receipts and rejects older revisions. At 256 requests in one room, new visual receipts are suppressed to prevent eviction-and-replay. Queue semantics, counts, floor transforms, idle clips and HTML text handling come from the approved baseline.
