## Synchronization

The SliceDriver provides transient ordered accepted effect records with the current room, revision and connection epoch. The audio director consumes these records and waits for the same Engine request, actor and seated Viewer target to enter confirmed `GIFT_FOCUS`. Observed/progress gifts remain silent. Additional accepted finals for the same focus can coalesce before speech and never restart speech already played. The audio deadline starts from the Engine's `focusStarted` time, uses the existing 4,800 ms base hold as a conservative bound, and cuts at an actual focus exit. It deliberately does not spend any scheduler extension on a new voice. Camera and gift counts remain Engine-owned.

The mixer renders one voice at a time on independent voice, gifts and ambience buses. During speech, the respective gifts and ambience gains settle at 48% and 28% of their operator settings. Release uses a 100 ms time constant. A master gain, compressor and peak ceiling protect the mix. Gift IDs map to separate original rose and lantern chimes or a neutral tone. The wind/water bed is one original bounded filtered-noise loop. Pending requests are capped at 20, the per-room replay ledger at 256 and simultaneous SFX oscillators at 24. The no-seat path offers a short non-spatial thank-you during an eligible cinematic gap and expires after 2,200 ms. In the capacity DEMO, D arrives during A's focus and expires before a gap.

A Web Lock grants one output owner per localhost origin. The click starts the AudioContext under a real gesture. Decoding and playback begin only after ownership. A second tab is blocked. Mute, hidden tab, WebGL context loss, page exit and session changes cut audio and clear pending work. Resuming with a gesture uses a fresh watermark. `source=live`, inspect and frozen-time modes keep audio inactive. No change is required to the PR #20 localhost/CORS/WebSocket configuration.

## Voice provenance

The original fixed Vietnamese WAV payloads come from the Owner-authorized V1 reuse at `9ffa2718723b11284dccdf35595c759576672f93`. The V1 manifest records the Owner's approval of the rose voice. This scoped DEMO uses fixed phrases from the same V1 corpus. The gTTS library license and Google's general terms were checked separately. A broader service-output redistribution or commercial grant was not established. The raw reference clip was unavailable for listening, and its soundtrack or music is absent from the mix. [V1 audio manifest](https://github.com/nguyenbui5701/OnlinePagoda/blob/9ffa2718723b11284dccdf35595c759576672f93/apps/overlay/public/audio/vi-v2/AUDIO_MANIFEST.md) · [gTTS MIT library license](https://gtts.readthedocs.io/en/latest/license.html) · [Google terms](https://policies.google.com/terms)

Runtime voice files contain a JSON envelope around the WAV bytes. This follows the approach used by V1 after empty media responses and reproduces the original PCM byte-for-byte for rose, lantern and generic. The Viewer verifies the unwrapped byte count and SHA-256 before Web Audio decoding. The three static originals are 4.272, 5.016 and 3.888 seconds respectively. V1's fixed generator applied volume 0.8. The Viewer plays the first 2.10 seconds of the decoded fixed phrase, ending in the pause before its second sentence. The short generic file is a separate offline FFmpeg derivation of the first sentence with `atempo=1.5` and an ending fade, 1.40425 seconds after encoding. This compares the speeds on the same source phrase. It does not prove the dynamic service. [Voice pins and FFmpeg filter](src/audio/voice-pins.json)

**`DYNAMIC_TTS_BLOCKED`**. New gTTS synthesis and named An/Bình/Chi/Duyên speech await verified service terms and separate approval for the synthetic generation step. This implementation uses the fixed, nameless voice while the existing UI still shows the correct actor name. Playback requires no Internet, Python or FFmpeg. FFmpeg is needed only to reproduce the evidence recording or the local short derivation. No external TTS worker is part of this PR.
