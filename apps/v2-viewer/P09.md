## Implementation and checks

The imported Sakura scene pivot at the trunk base defines the world X/Z target. The actor's measured +Z is rotated around world Y in pad-parent coordinates. The pad world matrix and seat contact stay fixed. The native idle drives the same three presentation anchors. Gift cameras derive their near-front viewpoint from that actor's world transform, using a 50° vertical lens to fit the full body and pad inside the capture margin.

Four authored closed splines share the same neighboring knots at the cycle boundary. Their east/west sweeps and low/high choices are selected once per cycle. The seed chooses the initial sweep and each cycle's elevation. Sweep direction alternates to avoid immediate repeats. The camera moves through CLOSE, COMMUNITY, AERIAL and UNDER_TREE. Position and lens curves share their boundary tangent. Eased orientation interpolation keeps the view axis continuous even when moving from the trunk to the foreground. The startup check samples clearance, subject distance and motion for every route. A rejection is reported in diagnostics, and selection falls back to an accepted route. The unchanged gift scheduler supplies the active actor. Return blends from the live pose to the opening of the current cinematic cycle.
