# P06c zero-cost original hair/scalp repair provenance

P06c is an original locally scripted corrective sculpt performed solely on the previously project-owned P06b male short-hair geometry. It did NOT download external 3D models/textures, acquire celebrity IP, use paid AI generation, fal, Meshy, Tripo, subscriptions, or marketplace assets. Geometry is derived from original SHA-locked P06b p6b/ two GLB files, by ray intersection of their original project-owned skin/hair mesh. Three short swept lock surfaces retained; no other original geometry changed, no material texture generated. Node.js v22 standard-library, Blender 4.5 LTS (owner-run source) and PowerShell, zero paid model/tool calls.

Underlying P06b assets inherit their project owner's existing rights and provenance; this file does not invent a new license grant over earlier source. Editable Blender scenes generated from actual imported original GLBs after owner execution. Spending on paid APIs = **0 VND**.
