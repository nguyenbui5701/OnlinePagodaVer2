# Sakura Hero R16 provenance

Original Sakura-only procedural refinement of R15 generated geometry. Zero-cost Python, numpy, trimesh, SciPy, scikit-image, VTK and optional free Blender on owner's Windows computer. No third-party model or purchased textures. R14 and R15 original geometry/assets are unmodified, ECO is byte-identical. No character, lotus lake, Event Engine, Codex or other V1/V2 application work. The owner may reuse generated original geometry subject to third-party library licenses. Production approval and merge are withheld pending SA.
